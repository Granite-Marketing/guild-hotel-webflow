Eva Struble is a San Diego-based artist whose work examines landscape through social, environmental, and personal histories. Her practice explores human-altered environments, regional botany, and the ways nature and human intervention continually reshape one another.

‘oceano’

by eva struble

2024 | 48 x 60 in
Oil and acrylic on canvas

Eva’s work unfolds through layered color, texture, and abstraction, conjuring the atmospheric qualities of a coastal landscape without settling into a singular representation of place. The work speaks to the deep relationship between landscape and the daily life of Southern California, where the natural environment is not simply a backdrop, but integral of how the region is experienced, remembered, and understood.
