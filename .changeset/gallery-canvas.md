Gallery canvas: the /gallery and /arts photo grids become a full-screen board you drag to move and zoom in three steps (zoom out, normal, zoom in). The page still scrolls past it with the wheel or a swipe; cmd or ctrl plus the wheel, or a trackpad pinch, zooms. On touch screens a joystick and the zoom buttons move the board. Photos keep their natural shape in brick rows, the filter re-lays out the board, and Arts pieces still open their detail pop-up (a drag never opens one). Needs the GSAP 3.15.0 Draggable and InertiaPlugin tags in the site footer.
