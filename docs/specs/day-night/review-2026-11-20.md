# Day and night: independent live check (2026-11-20)

Checked on https://the-guild-hotel-c34e71.webflow.io against Stephen's notes DN1-DN6 and the 21 tagged blocks (plus the Coronado Beach pop-up). Live site only; no PRs, logs or Designer used. Read-only.

How it was checked: real Chrome, desktop window 1512 wide (the window could not be shrunk to 1440, so desktop is 1512 not 1440), and 390 wide measured in a 390px frame of the same site so the phone layout rules applied. Each page: cookie cleared, fresh load, press Night, compare every tagged element before and after, then reload and navigate with the cookie set. A "tagged block" counts as having night content only when at least one night field has a value.

Honest limits: the 390 check used a 390px frame, not a phone and not a real tap. Console errors were read from the moment my console reader attached, so errors in the first instant of a page load could be missed. Editor-side field names (DN5) are in the Designer, which I was told not to open.

## Items

### 1 Proven
- **DN1 content only, not colours.** Pressing Night changed only text and images. Page and text colours were identical before and after, and the site stylesheet has no night colour rules.
- **DN1 cookie.** Pressing Night sets `guild-mode=night` (Day sets `guild-mode=day`). Navigating to other pages, and reloading, stayed on night with the Night button shown as active.
- **DN2 always day first.** With no cookie, every page loaded day (`is-day`) with day content.
- **DN3 fall back to day.** On every page, tagged blocks with no night content kept their day text and images; none went blank (0 blanks on all 6 pages at both widths).
- **DN6 image, heading, text.** Night image (hero, room cards) and night heading and paragraph text all swap correctly.
- **No flash of day content.** Loading a page with the night cookie keeps the filled elements hidden until the night version is in, then shows night. Day content was never visible first (tested on Home and Neighbourhood).
- **No console errors** on Home, Dining, room page and Neighbourhood (see limits).

### 2 Not done
- **DN5 twin fields (not checked).** I cannot see the editor field names without the Designer. On the live page every tagged element carries night slots (text, image, video, link) and empty ones fall back, which is consistent with "Night - <Field>" twins.
- **DN6 button text and link, and video.** No tagged button anywhere has night text or link filled, and no night video is filled anywhere. The swap for these could not be proven because there is nothing to swap.

### 4 Look at these (taste)
- **DN4 crossfade length.** Measured: fade out about 0.3s, text and image swap at about 0.35s, fully back by about 0.5 to 0.6s. That matches "about half a second". First time a night image loads it can hold the block blank a little longer (about 1s seen once).

## 3 Differs from what Stephen asked
1. **Tags beyond the list.** Night slots also exist on blocks Stephen did not list: Meetings hero, three Meetings pop-up panels, the Neighbourhood article cards (6 slots), the Neighbourhood second pop-up, and every room card on the Rooms listing. All are empty so nothing visibly differs, but editors will see extra night fields.
2. **One night image everywhere.** Every filled night image (Home, Rooms, Cozy King, Neighbourhood hero) is the same single picture, so it reads as a placeholder, not real per-block night photography. Night headings and text also look like test copy.
3. **Placeholder day copy on Meetings and Neighbourhood.** The day text that night replaces on the Meetings card and the Coronado pop-up is Latin filler ("Nullam scelerisque...", "Suspendisse viverra..."). Not a day/night fault but visible while comparing.
