## Stephen's question: is tab-pills-intro the same on every page?

Same component, same three variants (Base, Press, Media), five instances: /packages, /blog, /happenings, /press and the Post Categories template. Each instance has the same hand-typed props (Tab 1 to 4 label and link, Show tab 4) with different typed values. The real tabs on the live pages come from the CMS at runtime on all four listing pages:
- /press shows Reviews, Lists, F&B, Design, Community while its props say press, design, community.
- /happenings shows 2 tabs (dining, culture) while its props list 3.
- /blog and /packages show dining, culture, conferences (the Topics collection), which happen to equal the typed props.

So the pills are CMS-driven where they ship, but the Designer shows dead hand-typed props that look real and do nothing. Wellness, Meetings and Neighborhood do not use the component at all: they have hand-built native pill bars (Wellness: four static links, all `#`).
