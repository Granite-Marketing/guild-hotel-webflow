**THE GUILD COLLECTION**    
The works of eight artists have been chosen for The Guild Collection, art that speaks to the shifting nature of the coast and to maritime culture. Oils, acrylic, rope, clay, found objects and metals bring an arresting contemporary beauty to the historic Guild.
