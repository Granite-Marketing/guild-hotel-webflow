**Bold Promise, Bright Future**  
As a historic YMCA and now The Guild, our destination has been the social heart of San Diego since 1924

**Proud Stewards of City Spirit** 

**A Great Escape for the Services**  
500 West Broadway was built when San Diego’s identity was tightly bound to its waterfront and naval presence. As a YMCA, it became a destination of welcome and belonging for a growing military community, offering lodging, recreation, education and fellowship. Today, we’re proud stewards of that generous spirit of service, camaraderie and hospitality. 

**George Marston, The Founder**  
In 1924, George Marston saw more than a building. He imagined a grand center of city life, a gathering place to elevate hospitality and raise a downtown. The landmark he built became a magnet, welcoming and entertaining some eight million Army and Navy men and women over five decades.  

**“Once Opened, Never Closed”**  
The key to the YMCA was ceremoniously tossed into the Pacific on opening day in November 1924

**1924**  
**A Landmark YMCA is Born**  
In 1924, following San Diego’s 1915 Panama-California Exhibition, George Marston commissioned architects Lincoln Rogers and Frank Stevenson to design a building that would become a turning point in the city’s architectural history.  

The Italian Renaissance Revival structure they created had a symmetrical composition, richly articulated masonry, and generously proportioned public spaces. The message? Here is permanence, dignity, and civic generosity. Some things never change. 

**1924-1970**  
**Home to the Services**   
A postcard from the YMCA dated April 1945 bears the tagline “The Home Away From Home”.  In nearly five decades as a YMCA, some eight million Army and Navy servicemen and women called it home, playground, living room, bowling alley, restaurant, boxing ring, checkers table, and much, much more. If you listen carefully, the walls of The Guild will tell a half-century of stories of joyous community, intimacy and connection. 

**2019**  
**The Guild Opens its Doors**  
After the YMCA closed its doors, the downtown landmark slumbered until it was reborn as The Guild, part of Marriott’s Tribute Portfolio. As a luxury hotel, it provided an oasis for the ever-growing number of visitors to “America’s Finest City.”  

**2026**  
**A Radical Reimagining**  
With a transformative makeover, The Guild takes its rightful place as the heart of city hospitality — a destination hotel, resort and oasis in one. The beauty of the exterior architecture remains intact, while a new pool invites lounging by the water, and a one-of-a-kind art collection elevates public spaces. Exciting new restaurant offerings include California’s first Zuma, and rejuvenated rooms and meeting spaces offer refined, comfortable retreats. New wellness events, menus and fitness spaces make West Coast wellbeing an essential part of every visit. 

**A LANDMARK REIMAGINED**  
INC Architecture brought depth, material richness, and careful attention to craft to the rejuvenation of The Guild. The result is a destination that feels inseparable from its neighborhood, its history, and its future.
