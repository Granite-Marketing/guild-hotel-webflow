**GARDEN TERRACE**  
A leafy shelter for all-day alfresco dining. 

**FRESH AIR MEETS INSPIRED FLAVOR**  
The Garden Terrace brings the carefully prepared menu of the Lobby Bar into the breezy, shaded quiet of the courtyard. Generous breakfasts, from Buttermilk Pancakes and smoothies to Grand Slams, the king of morning offerings. Lunches, sharables and bites of all kinds nourish throughout the day. 

Peruse the drinks menu and start with a latte: Rose, Cardamom and Sesame would start the day on a creative footing. Société Brewing’s Pupil IPA cools the afternoon, and a proper Mezcal margarita kickstarts the night.      
    

**About the Space**  
Lush plantings, textured materials and carefully composed lighting make the Garden Terrace a calm retreat in which to start the day.

As in the original YMCA, the terrace offers a balance between community and sanctuary. Have it your way.   
   
**Group Dining & Events**   
Bring friends, colleagues and networking opportunities to the Garden Terrace. You, your friends and company can reserve part of the space for yourselves, or organize a full buyout.
