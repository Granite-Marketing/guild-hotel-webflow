**POOL LOUNGE**  
Hotel becomes resort at the sunny center of Guild life.   

**THE WATER’S FINE**  
Poolside life is best enjoyed with good friends and fabulous snacks. Our pool menu is hot, cold and satisfying, ranging from Watermelon Salad to Tacos de Jamaica, soft serve and beyond. Pull up a lounger and spend the afternoon. 

The Guild’s new Pool Lounge offers the best of many worlds – a buzzy social scene and a retreat from the city beyond our walls. Dip into cool calm in the morning. Wade into the action in the afternoon.    

**ABOUT THE SPACE**  
The Pool lounge is an urban haven and a comfortably chic gathering place from sunup until moonlight. The pool’s turquoise waters are framed by luxurious loungers, and high design wooden walls provide privacy. San Diego’s on your doorstep, but you’re a world away.  

**GROUP DINING AND EVENTS**   
Bring friends, colleagues and networking opportunities to the Pool Lounge. You, your friends and company can reserve part of the space for yourselves, or organize a full buyout.
