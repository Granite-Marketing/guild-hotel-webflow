**A Spirited History Meets a New Hospitality**

Behind this mannered Italian Renaissance facade is a century of glamorous mischief. Today, The Guild is an icon with open arms welcoming you to the heart of contemporary San Diego. 

**Pacific Coast Hospitality since 1924**

As the YMCA that welcomed Army and Navy men and women, our site was San Diego’s premier gathering place. As a luxury hotel, The Guild has curated, elevated and refined the experience – but at heart, we’re still a sailor on shore leave.
