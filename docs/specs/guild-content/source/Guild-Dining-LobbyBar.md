**LOBBY BAR**  
From breakfast ‘til late, you’ll find eats, drinks and comfortable conviviality

**HEART OF  THE GUILD**  
From early evening til nightcaps and all the Pandan Lattes, Wagyu Smash Burgers and Carajillos in between, the Lobby Bar is The Guild’s premier gathering place. The bar program, mentored by Dushan Zaric – founder of the acclaimed Employees Only – celebrates craftsmanship, quality and creativity. The all-day dining runs the gamut of healthy choices and temptations.    

**CHOICE BITS**  
Late night? You might need a Bloody Bull and a Brûléed French Toast. If comfort food calls at lunch, the Fried Chicken Sandwich or Grilled Cheese come to mind. Sharables will get the evening going, and if you like to get lost in a vast menu of Bourbon, Tequila or Scotch, you’re in the right place. 

**EXPLORE THE SPACE**  
When Inc. Architects designed the Lobby Bar, they moved from the bright welcome of the reception toward darker finishes and materials that define the bar as the social heart of the hotel. The Lobby Bar is an intimate, comfortably social setting – a space with a distinct, unmistakable identity.

**GROUP DINING AND EVENTS**   
Bring friends, colleagues and networking opportunities to the Lobby Bar. You, your friends and company can reserve part of the space for yourselves, or organize a full buyout.
