**ZUMA SAN DIEGO**

Welcome to the deliciously glamorous energy of California’s first Zuma

**CONTEMPORARY JAPANESE IZAKAYA**

Zuma is a feast for the senses: the open kitchen, sushi counter and robata grill turn out showstopping black cod, pork belly skewers, sea bass sashimi with yuzu, salmon roe and truffle. Try a signature dish – Condé Nast Traveler suggests the “rice hotpot with wild mushrooms and Japanese vegetables, or roasted lobster with shiso-ponzu butter”.  Peruse the menu of imaginative craft cocktails, too; your new favorite drink is there.  

Zuma has become a destination for sophisticated Japanese dining around the globe, a high-energy gathering place that celebrates the joys of sharing, tasting and conversation. From its origins in Knightsbridge, London, Zuma has conquered the world with a mix of polished glamor and spectacular flavors.

**EXPLORE THE SPACE**  
Your arrival at Zuma, through an airy, sculptural walkway framed in monkey pod wood from Thailand, is a one-of-a-kind taste of things to come. Our iconic illuminated sign and a textured boulder wall welcome you to a garden terrace framed by a wooden pergola and sheltered from the street. Everywhere, coastal influences and the rich hues of the San Diego sunset. There is only one Zuma. In California, it’s at The Guild.
