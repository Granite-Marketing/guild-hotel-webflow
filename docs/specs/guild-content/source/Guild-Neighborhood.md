**EXCITEMENT AT YOUR DOORSTEP**  
Step out for day and night dining, strolling, dancing, art, music and shows.  

**THE BEST OF SAN DIEGO**  
From The Guild, the city’s favorite neighborhoods and attractions are just a stroll away. The history, waterfront, culture, dining and nightlife of San Diego are yours to experience and savor from morning til late. 

**DOWNTOWN SAN DIEGO**  
It’s all here: the harbor and the Seaport Village; the Midway, America’s most famous ship; the Gaslamp Quarter, the epicenter of downtown entertainment and the soul of city nightlife; the surprising Petco Park, home of the beloved Padres; and boisterous, welcoming Little Italy. And many more places and experiences you don’t want to miss.    

Play is never far from work in San Diego, and our downtown is home to the best of all worlds – the Pacific waterfront, the city’s commercial heart, and a mix of locals, visitors and conference-goers. Our neighborhood brings together people, destinations and unforgettable sights.   

   
**GASLAMP QUARTER**  
San Diego is always on in the historic, dynamic Gaslamp, whose 16 blocks of streets are alive at all hours. The Gaslamp combines sunlit daytime strolls and gallery visits with joyous, after-dark celebrations that recall the vibrant festivities of New Orleans. Since the1860s, the Gaslight has seen it all.     

**LITTLE ITALY**  
At 48 walkable, delicious blocks, ours is the biggest Little Italy in the country, with a boisterous, welcoming heart to match. Visits to the Little Italy Mercato, our irresistible weekly farmer's market, inevitably lead to an aperitivo on one of countless patios, which invites more strolling, which might give you an appetite for an al fresco dinner. Little Italy is Europe in San Diego, a must.   

**MIDWAY MUSEUM**  
Aboard the mighty Midway – a 20-story behemoth three football fields long – visitors experience the sweep of modern naval history from the end of WWII to the 1990s Persian Gulf conflict. You’ll experience the life of a sailor and learn how the Midway was an instrument of both war and peace during its nearly 50 years of service.    

**BALBOA PARK**  
It’s a 1,200-acre park of countless dimensions, an adventure of your choosing: You can visit thousands of animals at the zoo; take a Tango lesson; bring the kids for a day they’ll never forget; take in the Botanical Garden and Lily Pond, one of the most photographed sites in the city; and see three museums, including one that explores the history of beer. And that’s just scratching the surface of this remarkable destination.

**CORONADO BEACH**  
The Coronado beaches and town are a postcard destination, with sparkling mica sands – the mica does that – and endless possibilities, from touring the canals to surfing and bodysurfing, soaking in tide pools, swimming in calm waters, or wandering for miles along the Pacific.    

**OCEANSIDE**  
Just 38 miles north of San Diego, Oceanside is the quintessential California beach town, with a 2,000-foot-long wooden pier where you can fish, enjoy the sunset or watch surfers. There’s a surf museum, a Thursday Sunset Market, and landmarks like the Mission San Luis Rey de Francia. Of course, you’ll also find the perfect spot to sunbathe or go for a swim.     

**PETCO PARK**  
It’s the home of the San Diego Padres, but it belongs to everyone. Petco Park, in the heart of downtown, has been named the best Major League Park in all of baseball multiple times. Game Day is great, but the park brings people together every day. Behind center field, grassy Gallagher Square is a family favorite, with a new kids’  play area designed around a 35-foot-tall baseball bat made of steel and wood. 

**RADY SHELL AT JACOBS PARK**  
The summer home of the San Diego Symphony is a premier bayside concert venue with exceptional acoustics. Besides classical works, the venue hosts movie concerts with a live orchestra, pop, rock, and contemporary headliners, as well as a range of jazz, electronic and world music.  

**SAN DIEGO MUSEUM OF ART**  
Located in Balboa Park, the SDMA is the biggest and most popular in the region. Its internationally renowned collection dates from 3000 BC to the present day, and its cultural and community experiences invite visitors to explore art through music, dance, film, food and much more. The museum is also home to the Museum of Photographic Arts.

**SEAPORT VILLAGE AND HARBOR**  
Steps from the harbor and convention center, the village is a destination for waterfront dining, shopping, and entertainment of all kinds, from a dockside market to a roller rink and a carousel with chariots, horses and other animals that has delighted visitors since 1895\.    

**SAN DIEGO CONVENTION CENTER**  
The Guild is proud to be a home away from home for attendees to the San Diego Convention Center, one of North America’s great meeting places. Few centers in the world are as perfectly situated: Its 615,701 sq. ft. of exhibit space overlooks both the city and the bay. Some 7,500 first-class hotel rooms are within a mile of its doors, and the center is within walking distance of the city’s best shopping and dining, including Horton Plaza, Seaport Village and the famed Gaslamp Quarter. PETCO Park, home of the Padres, is directly across from the center, and the airport is just 10 minutes away. 

**SUPERIOR COURT OF CALIFORNIA, COUNTY OF SAN DIEGO**   
The Guild regularly and securely hosts many firms researching cases that are tried at the Superior Court, which is less than a five-minute walk away. The court is part of the California trial court system, the largest of its kind in the world, and San Diego is the second largest court system in the state, after Los Angeles. The court’s activities are held in the 71 courtrooms of the San Diego Central Courthouse, which see criminal, civil, probate, family law, and small claims cases.  

**U.S. DISTRICT COURT FOR THE SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA**  
The Guild is just two blocks away from this court, and securely hosts firms researching cases that are tried there. Because of San Diego’s proximity to Mexico and its major land ports of entry, the court handles a high volume of immigration-related criminal prosecutions, drug trafficking cases, and border enforcement matters as well as standard civil litigation.

**HALL OF JUSTICE**   
The Guild is a three-minute walk from the Hall of Justice, and the hotel has become a trusted host for firms working on cases to be tried there. The Hall of Justice contains courtrooms for the Superior Court of California \- County of San Diego, the San Diego County District Attorney’s Office, the Sheriff’s Court Services Bureau, Probation, Adult Forensic Services, the Public Defender, and the U.S. Attorney.
