# Ticket 230: scroll jumps near the footer and when FAQ items open or close (M6, M8 note)

## Diagnosis

**What moved the page:** the browser's own scroll anchoring, not Lenis and not a ScrollTrigger refresh.

- The accordion animates `max-height` for 0.4s. Opening one item closes the open one, so two items change height at once.
- Chrome-family browsers (scroll anchoring on by default) shift `scrollY` to keep an on-screen element still while an element above it changes height. With two items moving at once, and the page end clamping the position, the adjustments do not cancel: the position ends hundreds of pixels away from where it started.

**Evidence**

- Fixture (390px wide, scrolled to the page end, open item 3 while item 1 closes), no Lenis at all (`?engine=none`): scroll position 1285 to 1033, a loss of 252px, with the document height identical before and after (2085). With Lenis it is the same (1285 to 1033).
- Same fixture with `overflow-anchor: none` on `html`: scroll position stays at 1285 for the whole transition.
- Staging at v1.2.35, 390px, touch emulation, read-only: at the page end, opening a FAQ item moves scroll forward by 126px over 7 frames (+7 to +25 per frame) and then back by 126px (-15 decaying to -1). Mid-page (FAQ in view) no scroll change at all. This matches M6: the skip shows near the footer after the FAQ, and the "small skips" on open and close.
- Refreshes per toggle on staging: exactly 1, 600 to 900ms after the click (after the transition, because `refresh(true)` waits for scroll to settle). Scroll position does not move at the refresh. So the refresh layer is not the cause.

**Do the CLAUDE.md refresh rules still hold?** Yes. Nothing in the diagnosis contradicts them: refresh stays `ScrollTrigger.refresh(true)`, never from a scroll handler or ticker, and it fired once per toggle. `lenis.resize()` on every refresh is unchanged.

**M8 footer jitter (note only, not fixed here):** while the accordion height animates, triggers below the FAQ (the booking and newsletter reveals, the footer scale) are positioned for the old height until the single refresh 600 to 900ms later. Together with the scroll movement above, that makes scrubbed footer motion step. With scroll no longer moving, the stale window shows no scroll-driven change. Whether the footer image scale still looks jittery after this is for the live check, and M8's one-image footer is a separate ticket.

## Fix

`src/utils/accordion.ts`: every toggle sets `overflow-anchor: none` on `document.documentElement` and removes it 700ms later (longer than the 0.4s transition), cleared and restarted on rapid clicks. Anchoring works as normal the rest of the time (late images, CMS hydration). Fixed once, in the shared module, so every accordion on every page gets it.
