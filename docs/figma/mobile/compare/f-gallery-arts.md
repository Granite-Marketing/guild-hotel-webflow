## Shared components

- `section_hero-fullbleed-centered`: Live hero is ~843px tall with ~22px title and ~16px body; Figma ~800px, ~20px title, ~14px body. Seen on gallery and arts (low). Same top bar mismatch (see site-nav).
- `site-nav`: live shows "BOOK NOW" text link on the right of the top bar and no moon/sun theme toggle; Figma shows the toggle and no Book Now (nav-mobile frames describe the Book Now sheet separately). Seen on gallery and arts (med).
- `tab-pills-intro_tabs is-scroll` / `section_gallery-all`: filter UI differs from Figma on both pages (gallery uses a compact dropdown pill in Figma; live uses a scrolling pill row overlapping the grid). Tile gutters 20px Figma vs ~12px live (high).
- `section_offers-slider-split`: image is not nested between title and body inside the maroon block, instead appears as a separate full-bleed block below it, leaving a large empty gap in the slide; title is larger and slide is much taller than Figma. Seen on gallery and arts (high).
- `section_card-carousel-header-left`: extra prev/next arrow row under the paragraph, narrower cards, larger heading. Seen on gallery and arts (med).
- `section_faq-accordion`: 32px side gutter vs 20px Figma, larger question and answer type (16px vs 14 to 15px). Seen on gallery and arts (med).
- `section_cta-booking-newsletter` (and `cta-booking-newsletter_actions`): email and JOIN US are inline on one row with a divider between the two blocks; Figma stacks full-width email above a centred JOIN US with no divider. Seen on gallery and arts (high).
- `section_footer-links` (`footer-links_columns`): link pitch ~29px vs ~44px, 32px vs 20px gutter, TRIBUTE PORTFOLIO mark left and small vs centred ~28px, no rule above brand block, extra BLOG / HAPPENINGS / SOLANA COFFEE links. Seen on gallery and arts (high).
