## Variants

Webflow combos style one element, so each modifier goes on every element it changes. In Webflow these become component variants (open question 1).

- **Base** (4 instances, identical content). Cream section, tilted script heading (rotate -8deg) on the left, paragraph and chamfered button on the right, 1291x881 map illustration below (`aspect-ratio: 1291 / 881`, 6rem gap). Tablet and below: heading, text and button stack.
- **`is-image-bg`** (4 instances, identical content) on `section_map-feature`, `map-feature_content`, `_header`, `_aside`, `_figure`, `_image`. Desktop: section keeps `aspect-ratio: 1600 / 1170`, the map photo covers it (grayscale, 10% darker, as the Figma fills do), the content becomes a 485px brown (`#906E58`) card pinned 2.5rem from the bottom-left with an H4 heading, paragraph and four place rows, and four 68px pins sit at % positions. Tablet and below: map on top at the same aspect (pins still line up), card full width underneath.
- **`is-centered`** (1 instance) on `map-feature_content`, `_header`, `_aside`, `_figure`, `_image`. Centred H2 and paragraph, illustration below at `aspect-ratio: 1523 / 865`, 95.19rem max width, `object-position: 50% 80%`, `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so its white ground takes the cream.

## Open questions

1. Per-instance class differences need Webflow component variants (one per plan variant). X01 should confirm MCP can set classes per variant; the fallback is one component with visibility-switched wrappers, never a second component.
2. The base map SVG is not in the Webflow asset library. Upload it before the Designer session.
3. The base map art shows Atlanta streets and "Hotel Clermont". It looks like placeholder art from another project. Ship as is or replace?
4. `#906E58` has no colour variable (D01 added only #CB3629 and #4A1217). Add `color-brand-906e58` or keep the hex.
5. The history illustration is masked in Figma by a freeform vector (236:8252) that hides the inset key map and compass. The rectangular crop still shows their lower parts. Export the masked group 236:8254 as a transparent PNG, or accept.
6. The four "Directions" links have no URLs. Supply Google Maps links.
7. "Discover more" links to `/neighborhood` in the plan. Confirm.
8. No mobile design: the base map shrinks to about 326px wide at 390, with unreadable labels. Accept or supply a mobile crop.
9. legal and corporate are CMS template pages (Q028); their instances go in there.

## Deviations

See `deviations` in [plan.json](plan.json). In short: history is planned as `is-centered` only (its illustration sits below the header, not behind it); `is-1-image` is dropped because it would carry no style; pins are CSS circles; the map card's divider colour is read from the crop.

## Compare notes

A Sonnet subagent compared each 1440 preview with its crop (Figma 1600 wide; crops are 0.75x for base and image-bg, about 0.66x for history; the previews are 0.9x). Its findings, with my corrections where it misread the scale or the crop:

### Base: `home/12-the-neighborhood` (minor differences)

- Matches: cream ground, same map art and colours, script title top left with the text and chamfered button top right, map full width below and cropped at the bottom as in Figma.
- Differs: the title renders wider than Figma (Snell Roundhand fallback is wider than Morello) and the intro text wraps to two lines (Avenir Next is wider than Gatch Test), which pushes the button about 25px lower. Map top sits about 40px lower relative to the title. Expected to close once the real fonts load; the 6rem title-to-map gap is the value to retune in the Designer if the live font still leaves a gap.

### `is-image-bg`: `meetings-collection/07-steps-from-key-legal-addresses` (minor differences)

- Matches: grayscale map covering the section, four brown pins on the same courthouses, brown card at bottom left with the H4, paragraph and four ruled place rows in Figma order and copy, card colour.
- Differs: the H4 fits on one line (Copperplate fallback is narrower than Sackers); Figma wraps it to two, which also makes the card about 30px taller there. The subagent read the card as about 12% larger, but at matched scale it measures 485x623 Figma px, the spec values. Body copy size follows the fallback font.

### `is-centered`: `history/08-discover-the-property` (major differences, from the image crop)

- Matches: centred H2 and two-line paragraph, illustration below on cream (multiply blend), legend at bottom right.
- Differs: Figma masks the illustration with a freeform shape that hides the inset key map and compass; the plan's rectangular crop leaves their lower parts visible at the top (open question 5). The subagent also reported the heading and paragraph about 10% larger and the image about 25px closer to the paragraph; both come from fallback font metrics and the 2.75rem gap, to retune with the live fonts.

### 390 (no Figma mobile design)

No layout bugs: no overflow or clipped text. Base stacks title, text, button, then a small map (open question 8). Image-bg puts the map on top at the 1600/1170 aspect with pins aligned and the card full width below. Centered wraps the heading to two lines and shows the illustration below.
