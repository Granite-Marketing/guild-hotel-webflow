George Marston

In 1924, George Marsden saw more than a building. He imagined a grand center of city life, a gathering place to elevate hospitality and raise a downtown. The Italian Renaissance icon he built welcomed and entertained some eight million servicemen and women.

The Guild preserves the existing structure, retains defining architectural elements, and incorporates materials that will age gracefully,  extending the life of this important civic landmark.
